Key Notes
Keeping it useful: short entries beat long ones. Dates and amounts beat adjectives. If it involves money, log it the day it happens. If it involves the kids, log what changed — not how you felt about it. The Companion pack's What to write down guidance covers what's worth recording and what's overkill.
