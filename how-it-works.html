What Atlas does

Six questions. One tool each.

The things that feel impossible to hold in your head all at once.

Today

Where am I, and what needs me first?

Pick your phase. Your next step, tasks and scripts change to match where you actually are.

Open Today → Money

What money do I actually have?

Map what comes in, what goes out and what’s left. No spreadsheet skills needed.

Open Money → Week

What does the week look like?

Custody, handovers, meals and routines, so the week stops living in your head.

Open Week → Records

What happened, and when?

Payments, incidents, messages and changes, dated in one place. Print a clean PDF when someone official asks.

Open Records → Admin

What admin can’t slip?

Documents, accounts, school, medical and legal tasks that get expensive when they’re missed.

Open Admin → Goals

What am I rebuilding toward?

Goals, routines and next actions for when you’re ready to look forward.

The weekly reset

Ten minutes, once a week.

Done consistently, this turns Atlas from an app into a habit.

  1. Check your phaseSame phase, or has something shifted? Thirty seconds, and it sets up everything else.
  2. Open TodayGet the headline for money, the week, admin and goals before the week begins.
  3. Update your weekCustody, meals, handovers, events. Anything that’s changed since last week.
  4. Review moneyAnything urgent? Any new expenses to log? One look is enough to stay across it.
  5. Clear urgent adminTick off what’s done. Add what’s new. Keep the list honest.
  6. Pick 1–3 next actionsNot a to-do list. Just what matters this week. Three is plenty.

Once a week. Before the week starts. Ten minutes at most. That’s the whole system.

Where to begin

Start where you are.

Find your phase and tap it. Today, Admin and Scripts change to match.
